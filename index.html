What is DEEP?

DEEP / 1000 is an educational, non-commercial visual archive that brings together 1,000 astronomical image records from publicly accessible NASA resources. Instead of presenting a conventional thumbnail gallery, it arranges the collection in one continuous WebGL field and requests full media and metadata only when a visitor opens an object. Every published record preserves the source and supplied credit. Source-checked editorial guides explain what to observe, how color was produced and why a feature matters, while guided Trails connect selected images around ideas such as scale, morphology, wavelength and instrumentation. The project was created by Daniel García Silva as a frontend performance study. DEEP is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by NASA. Some material hosted by NASA may belong to third parties and remains subject to its respective rights and usage terms; editorial guidance does not claim specialist scientific review.
